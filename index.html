Wu Research Group

Institute of Frontier Chemistry, School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering · Shandong University

High-throughput polymer synthesis
Self-driving polymer blend discovery
Scheduling methods for automated experiments

We are an experimental group at the Institute of Frontier Chemistry, School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Shandong University, working at the intersection of chemistry, polymer science, lab automation, and machine learning. We develop and apply technologies to accelerate the discovery of synthetic polymers and understand biological systems, with an emphasis on generating scalable, high-quality data from experiments. We are currently interested in random heteropolymers and de novo designed proteins for biocatalysis.

We value interdisciplinary research and collective growth, and are actively recruiting from a range of backgrounds in chemistry, polymer science, protein engineering, robotics, and machine learning. Whether you come from the experimental or the computational side, if you want to learn things outside what you already know, we would like to hear from you!
