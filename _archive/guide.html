Group Guide

This guide contains practical information for current and prospective group members — onboarding procedures, lab protocols, computing resources, and useful contacts.

Getting Started

Welcome to the Wu Research Group. When you first join, please set up your university credentials, request access to shared lab spaces, and schedule an onboarding meeting with Guangqi. You will also be added to our group Slack and shared drive.

Lab Safety & Protocols

All group members must complete the university's chemistry safety training before beginning lab work. Standard operating procedures for our high-throughput platforms and nanopore setups are documented in the shared drive. If you are unsure about any procedure, ask before proceeding.

Computing & Data

We use GitHub for code, a shared server for data storage, and Notion for project tracking. Please keep raw data, analysis scripts, and experiment logs organized and backed up. Ask for access credentials during your onboarding meeting.

Group Meetings

We hold weekly group meetings on Fridays. Each member gives a short update every two weeks. Literature discussions and coding clinics rotate monthly. Remote attendance is always possible — just let us know in advance.
