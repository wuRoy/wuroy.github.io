Guangqi Wu is a professor at the Institute of Frontier Chemistry, School of Chemistry and Chemical Engineering, Shandong University, where he leads a research group. His research sits at the intersection of polymer chemistry, automation, and artificial intelligence, focusing on data-driven discovery of functional polypeptides and random heteropolymers with protein-like functions. He earned his Ph.D. in Polymer Chemistry and Physics from Peking University (2020) under the supervision of Prof. Hua Lu. He obtained his B.S. in Pharmacy from Ocean University of China (2015). 

Prior to joining Shandong University, he was a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Oxford (2024–2026), working with Prof. Yujia Qing and Prof. Hagan Bayley on nanopore-based polymer sequencing. Before that, he worked as a Postdoctoral Associate at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (2022–2024) under the supervision of Prof. Connor W. Coley, where he developed autonomous platforms for polymer discovery. He also held a Boya Postdoctoral Fellowship at Peking University (2020–2022) under the supervision of Prof. Hua Lu. He is a Lindau alumnus, having been selected to attend the 74th Lindau Nobel Laureate Meeting in Chemistry (2025).
